Privacy Policy — Sushi Toy Box

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sushi Toy Box ("the App"), developed by Iwao Games, handles your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files, and does not transmit any data over the internet.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress (coins, unlocked levels, and toy collection) only in local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If advertising is added in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly before release.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: sushi-toybox@outlook.jp

プライバシーポリシー(日本語)

ゲームの進行状況(コイン・レベル・コレクション)は、お使いの端末内にのみ保存され、アプリを削除すると消去されます。現バージョンでは広告・解析等の第三者サービスを使用していません(将来広告を導入する場合は、公開前に本ポリシーを更新します)。
